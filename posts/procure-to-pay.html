Understanding the Procure-to-Pay (P2P) Cycle in D365 F&O

Accounts payable Published Aug 26, 2026 Aayush Tiwari 10 min read
▾Overview

The P2P cycle is a crucial process in any organization, ensuring smooth operations from procurement to payment. Here's a breakdown of the key events and associated accounting entries in D365 F&O.

▾Requisition through purchase order
  • Create and submit a purchase requisition for approval
  • Approve the requisition and convert it to a purchase order
  • Request vendor quotations where no active trade agreement exists, then compare and approve
  • Create and validate the purchase order, submitting for approval if required
  • Approve the PO and confirm it to the vendor, then receive vendor acknowledgment
▾Receipt of goods or services

Record the receipt of goods or services via a product receipt.

Accounting: Debit Un-invoiced Inventory / Un-invoiced Expense Account  |  Credit Accrued Liabilities / GRNI (Goods Received Not Invoiced) Account.

Physical movement details — physical voucher, physical date, physical cost of inventory — are recorded at this stage.

▾Invoice processing & approval

Receive and enter the vendor invoice, performing three-way matching between the purchase order, product receipt, and vendor invoice. Submit for approval.

Accounting (reversal of the GRN entry):

  • Debit: Invoiced Inventory / Invoiced Expense Account
  • Debit: Input Taxes
  • Credit: Accounts Payable

Financial movement details — financial voucher, financial date, financial cost of inventory — are recorded here.

▾Payment processing & execution

Schedule the payment, generate and validate the payment proposal, then execute payment via the chosen method.

Accounting: Debit Accounts Payable  |  Credit Cash/Bank Account.

Finally, reconcile bank statements with recorded payments, generate reports on procurement and payment activities, and close the accounting period.
