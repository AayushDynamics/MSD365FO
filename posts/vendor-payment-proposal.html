Vendor Payment Proposal in D365 Finance

Accounts payable Published Sep 1, 2026 Aayush Tiwari 7 min read
▾Overview

One of the most underrated yet powerful tools in Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations is the Vendor Payment Proposal process. For anyone in Accounts Payable, managing vendor payments can feel like juggling due dates, discounts, payment methods, and approval timelines — the payment proposal feature is where automation steps in.

In simple terms, it's a way to automatically select vendor invoices for payment based on predefined criteria: due date or cash discount date, payment method or currency, vendor group, and legal entity. Instead of manually scanning hundreds of invoices, the system picks the ones that are due — letting finance teams focus on review and approval.

▾How it works in D365 F&O
  • Navigate to: Accounts Payable > Payments > Vendor payment journal
  • Create a new journal or select an existing one, then go to lines
  • Click Payment proposal → Create payment proposal
  • Define filters — e.g. all invoices due before the 10th, or eligible for early payment discounts
  • Review the proposed list and make adjustments if needed
  • Generate payments via bank file, check, or another configured method

The system settles invoices automatically once payments are posted — streamlining the entire pay-run process.

▾Real-world benefits
  • Efficiency & automation — proposals automatically pick due or discount-eligible invoices, cutting processing time
  • Cash flow optimization — plan disbursements strategically, take early discounts or defer non-urgent payments
  • Error reduction — system-driven logic lowers the risk of duplicate or missed payments
  • Standardization & control — consistent, rule-based selection with clear audit trails
  • Better vendor relationships — on-time, accurate payments build supplier trust
  • Visibility — the proposal report shows who's getting paid, when, and how
▾Implementation notes

Having implemented this across automobile, beauty, jewelry, and manufacturing clients, a few lessons stand out:

  • Set clear filters and criteria aligned with business rules
  • Ensure vendor master data (payment terms, methods, bank details) is clean before go-live
  • Introduce workflow approvals for payment journals to maintain accountability
  • Automate recurring proposals for clients running weekly or bi-weekly pay runs
  • Train AP users not to skip the review step — automation doesn't replace validation
