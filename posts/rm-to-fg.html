From Raw Materials to Finished Goods: A Comprehensive Journey in Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations!

Production control Published Aug 31, 2026 Aayush Tiwari 9 min
▾Post

In every manufacturing process, the transition from raw materials to finished goods involves critical stages, each contributing to operational and financial success. Here’s a breakdown of the key events in this cycle and the corresponding accounting entries in Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations (D365 F&O):

▾1. Production Planning


This stage sets the foundation for efficient resource allocation and scheduling:

Demand Forecasting: Analyse historical data to predict needs.

  • Master Planning (MRP): Generate planned orders for raw materials and production.
  • Production Order Creation: Define the Bill of Materials (BOM) and routing.

No financial entries are posted here, but these activities are vital for material reservation and readiness.

▾2. Production Execution


This stage converts planned activities into actionable tasks, focusing on material usage and cost recording:

  • Raw Material & Packing Material Picking list posting

Reserve and issue raw materials to production. Consumption of ray material is done by Pick list posting:

  • Accounting

Work In progress (Estimated) – Dr.

Raw Material Inventory (Estimated) – Cr.

Packing Material Inventory (Estimated) – Cr.

  • Job/Route Card Posting

Track labour hours, machine usage, and overhead costs.

  • Accounting

Work In progress (Estimated) – Dr.

Labor/Machine Costs (Estimated) – Cr.

▾3. Report production output


Report the completed quantity of production against the production order. This is done before the quality check stage to record progress and reflect partially or fully completed production:

  • Report as finished

Record the physical inventory of finished products.

  • Accounting

Finished Goods estimated cost- Dr.

Work In progress (Estimated) – Cr.

▾4.Quality Control


A stage focused on ensuring standards, without direct financial impacts. Quality assurance ensures that intermediate and finished goods meet predefined standards:

In-Process Inspections: Test WIP inventory to ensure quality during production.

Finished Goods Quality Check: Inspect products before adding to inventory.

No direct financial entries, but this step is key to maintaining quality and reducing wastage.

▾5. Completion and Costing


Finalize production by realizing actual finished goods inventory:

  • Production Order Ending:

Mark the production order as ended, transferring estimated cost to actual financial costs from WIP to finished goods. Financial inventory of finished goods are recorded at this state of production order.

  • Accounting

Reversal of Picking list, Route Cads & Report as finished Accounting

Raw Material Inventory – Cr.

Packing Material Inventory – Cr.

Labor/Machine Costs (Estimated) – Cr.

Work In progress – Dr.

Work In progress – Cr.

Finished Goods Inventory – Dr.

Each step plays a vital role in ensuring seamless operations and accurate financial reporting. Understanding the financial impact of these events can help manufacturers maximize efficiency and maintain compliance.
