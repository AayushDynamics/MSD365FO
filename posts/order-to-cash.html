Navigating the Order-to-Cash (O2C) Cycle in D365 F&O

Accounts receivable Published Aug 31, 2026 Aayush Tiwari 8 min read
▾Overview

The O2C cycle is a fundamental process in any organization, ensuring smooth operations from order placement to cash receipt. Here's a detailed breakdown of the key events and associated accounting entries in D365 F&O.

▾Order creation to inventory allocation
  • Create and validate a sales order based on customer requirements
  • Confirm the sales order with the customer
  • Allocate inventory for the confirmed sales order — a physical reservation
▾Picking, packing & invoicing

Pick items from inventory and pack for shipment. Optional accounting entries: Debit Cost of Goods Sold (delivered), Credit Inventory, Credit Deferred revenue on delivery, Debit Deferred revenue offset on delivery. Inventory movement is recorded using the moving average cost method.

Invoice generation calculates taxes and reverses the picking/packing entries:

  • Debit: Accounts Receivable (total invoice amount)
  • Credit: Sales Revenue (net sales amount)
  • Credit: Tax Payables
  • Debit: Cost of Goods Sold
  • Credit: Inventory
▾Shipping through payment reconciliation

Ship the packed items and confirm delivery with the customer. On payment processing:

  • Debit: Cash/Bank Account
  • Credit: Accounts Receivable

Payments are then reconciled against outstanding invoices, and receivables aging is tracked.

▾Reporting & period close

Generate reports on sales, invoices, and receivables, then finalize financial records by closing the accounting period. These accounting principles remain consistent across different ERP software.
