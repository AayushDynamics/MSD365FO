About

▾Welcome

Welcome to MS D365 F&O – Exploring Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations, a knowledge-sharing platform created for professionals, consultants, finance users, and anyone interested in understanding Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations (D365 F&O) from a practical and functional perspective.

▾About me

I'm Aayush Tiwari, a Senior Functional Consultant – Finance working with Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations.

With hands-on experience across D365 F&O implementations, migrations, support, finance transformation, and India localization, my primary area of expertise is the Finance side of Dynamics 365. My work involves translating business requirements into practical ERP solutions while working closely with Finance, Accounts, Tax, Supply Chain, and technical teams.

My professional journey has given me exposure to areas including:

  • General Ledger & Financial Management
  • Accounts Payable & Accounts Receivable
  • Procure-to-Pay (P2P)
  • Order-to-Cash (O2C)
  • Fixed Assets
  • Inventory & Accounting Integration
  • GST & India Localization
  • E-Invoicing & E-Way Bill
  • Posting Profiles & Accounting Entries
  • Data Migration & Reconciliation
  • SQL & D365 F&O Data Analysis
  • Reporting, Power BI & Excel
  • Functional Design, UAT & Implementation

I also hold a Master of Commerce (M.Com) in Finance, which helps me approach D365 F&O not only from a system perspective but also from the underlying accounting and business-process perspective.

▾Why this blog?

D365 F&O is a powerful platform, but understanding it effectively requires more than knowing which button to click.

A functional consultant often needs to understand:

Business Process → D365 Configuration → Transaction → Accounting Entry → Data → Reporting

That is the approach I want to bring to this blog.

Here, I aim to share practical explanations of D365 F&O concepts, implementation learnings, accounting flows, troubleshooting approaches, configuration insights, and technical knowledge that can help other professionals solve real-world problems.

▾What you'll find here

The content on this website will primarily focus on:

📌 D365 Finance
General Ledger, AP, AR, Fixed Assets, Cash & Bank, Financial Dimensions and more.

📌 Business Processes
P2P, O2C, Record-to-Report (R2R), inventory and other end-to-end ERP processes.

📌 Accounting & D365
Understanding vouchers, posting profiles, posting types, physical vs. financial postings, and the accounting impact of transactions.

📌 India Localization
GST, e-Invoicing, E-Way Bill and other India-specific D365 F&O requirements.

📌 Functional Consulting
Implementation approaches, functional design, UAT, data migration, requirement analysis and troubleshooting.

📌 Technical & Data Insights
SQL, D365 F&O tables, integrations, APIs, reporting and practical data analysis.

▾The goal

The goal of MS D365 F&O is simple:

To make complex D365 F&O concepts easier to understand through practical, real-world explanations.

Whether you're a D365 beginner trying to understand the fundamentals, a Finance professional learning ERP, a functional consultant preparing for your next project, or an experienced consultant looking for practical insights, I hope you find something useful here.

This blog represents my learning, experiences, and understanding of Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations. It is intended for knowledge sharing and does not represent the official views of Microsoft or any organization I work for.
